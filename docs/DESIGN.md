# DESIGN.md — Design System (Zynn: Elegant Simple Aksen Putih Lembayung Gold)

## 1. Konsep Desain: Elegant Simple Aksen Putih Lembayung Gold
- **Filosofi**: Ultra-clean, uncluttered, spacious, modern luxury minimalism.
- **Atmosphere**: Latar belakang putih lembayung bersih (subtle pearlescent glow) dengan aksen sentuhan emas (fine champagne & metallic gold strokes) yang berkelas dan proporsional.
- **Whitespace / Breathing Room**: Spasi vertikal yang lapang, hierarki visual yang sangat jernih, dan tidak padat.

## 2. Palet Warna
- **Canvas / Background**:
  - Base: `#FAF9F8` (Pristine Pearl)
  - Lembayung Aura: `rgba(238, 230, 245, 0.45)` & `rgba(255, 248, 235, 0.6)` (Subtle multi-dimensional soft glow)
- **Cards**:
  - `bg-white/90 backdrop-blur-xl`
  - Subtle Border: `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.18)` & hover `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.6)`
  - Soft Shadow: `0 10px 30px -10px rgba(180, 150, 80, 0.08)`
- **Aksen Emas (Gold Accents)**:
  - Gold Light: `#F3DC9B`
  - Gold Core: `#D4AF37`
  - Gold Deep: `#997517`
  - Metallic Gradient: `linear-gradient(135deg, #FDF7E7 0%, #E2C068 50%, #B88B22 100%)`
- **Tipografi**:
  - Text Main: `#1A191E` (Crisp Charcoal Black)
  - Text Muted: `#757185` (Neutral Slate)
  - Gold Accent Text: `#B5891C`

## 3. Tipografi & Proporsi
- Font Family: `Inter`, `Plus Jakarta Sans`, sans-serif
- Tracking: Subtle negative tracking pada heading (`tracking-tight`) untuk kesan modern dan bersih.
- Line Height & Weight: Headline medium/bold, subheadline ultra-clean regular.

## 4. Komponen & Kartu (4 Link)
- 4 Kartu didesain ramping, lapang, bertekstur kaca halus (frosted glassmorphism), dan interaktif saat hover/tap.
- Smooth spring animations & micro-haptics feeling.
